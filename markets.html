Yield curve

Latest closes plotted by maturity. An upward-sloping curve is normal; flat or inverted usually means investors expect slower growth or rate cuts ahead.

How to read this. Yields are quoted in percent (annualized). A move of one hundredth of a percent equals one basis point (bp) — so +5 bp means the yield rose by 0.05%. Red = yields rose that day (bad for existing bond holders and for mortgage rates); teal = yields fell.
